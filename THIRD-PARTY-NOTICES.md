## Iconoir

The app icon (`build/icon.svg`, rasterised to `build/icon.png`) and the Steam
library artwork are built from Iconoir's `refresh-double` glyph: recoloured,
rescaled and padded, with a gradient background and wordmark added. The glyph
itself is unchanged. It is not derived from any game or store artwork.

Iconoir is MIT licensed, Copyright (c) 2021 Luca Burgio. The full text is in
[`licenses/iconoir.txt`](licenses/iconoir.txt). Source:
<https://github.com/iconoir-icons/iconoir>.

## Fonts

The library artwork uses Segoe UI for the wordmark. It is not redistributed as
a font file; the generated PNGs are the only output.
